# Ticketverkauf – Graduation Ball Ticket Management System

## About the Project

A ticket management system for the graduation ball of HTL Leonding.

The platform provides a centralized solution for organizing ticket sales, managing bookings, reserving tables, and keeping track of attendees. Students, teachers, and graduates can register and purchase tickets online.

Graduates (Maturanten) receive early access to ticket sales before the general sale begins. The system aims to make the entire ticket-selling process simple, transparent, and efficient for both organizers and guests.

## Target Users

The platform is designed for four main user groups:

- **Graduates (Maturanten):** Receive early access to ticket sales and can purchase tickets before the general public.
- **Students:** Can register, browse available tickets, and purchase tickets during the regular sales period.
- **Teachers:** Can register and purchase tickets through the platform.
- **Organizers / Administrators:** Manage ticket sales, monitor payments, organize tables, and oversee all bookings.

## Main Features

### 1. User Registration & Authentication

- Registration and login for students, teachers, and graduates.
- Different user roles with different permissions.
- User profiles with personal information and booking history.
- Early access permissions for graduates.

### 2. Ticket Sales

- Online ticket selection and purchasing.
- Different ticket categories and prices, if required.
- Early ticket sales for graduates.
- General ticket sales for students and teachers.
- Real-time overview of available and sold tickets.
- Automatic ticket availability updates.

### 3. Shopping Cart

- Add tickets to a shopping cart.
- Change ticket quantities or remove tickets.
- Display the total price before checkout.
- Review the order before confirming the purchase.
- Prevent purchasing more tickets than are available.

### 4. Payment & Booking

- Bank transfer as the payment method.
- Display the school's or organizer's IBAN during checkout.
- Show the total amount and payment reference.
- Generate a booking confirmation after checkout.
- Payment status tracking (Pending, Paid, Cancelled).
- Organizers can manually confirm incoming payments.

*Note: Payments are initially handled through bank transfers rather than automatic online payment processing.*

### 5. Table Reservations

- View available tables and seating arrangements.
- Reserve tables together with ticket purchases.
- Display available and occupied seats.
- Allow guests to select a preferred table.
- Prevent multiple reservations for the same seats.
- Organizers can manage and modify table assignments.

### 6. Booking Management

- Overview of all customer bookings.
- View purchased tickets and reservation details.
- Track payment status.
- Cancel or modify bookings according to organizer rules.
- Access booking history.

### 7. Admin Dashboard

- Overview of total ticket sales and revenue.
- Monitor available, reserved, and sold tickets.
- Manage users and their roles.
- Confirm or reject payments.
- Manage ticket categories and prices.
- Manage tables and seating arrangements.
- View and export attendee lists.
- Search and filter bookings.

## Ticket Purchase Process

1. A user creates an account or logs in.
2. The system checks their user role and determines whether early access is available.
3. The user selects the desired number of tickets.
4. Tickets are added to the shopping cart.
5. The user can select a table or reserve seats.
6. The order summary displays all selected items and the total price.
7. The user confirms the booking.
8. The system displays the bank details, IBAN, payment reference, and amount.
9. The user transfers the money.
10. An organizer verifies the payment and updates the booking status.
11. Once the payment is confirmed, the tickets are marked as paid and the booking is completed.

- Simplify the organization of graduation ball ticket sales.
- Reduce manual administrative work.
- Make ticket availability and payment status transparent.
